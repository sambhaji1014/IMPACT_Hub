Volunteer Level Tiers

Volunteers earn 10 points for every confirmed service hour logged by partner NGOs, plus bonus points for first drives and streaks.

  • Bronze Changemaker: 0+ hours
  • Silver Advocate: 15+ confirmed hours
  • Gold Leader: 35+ confirmed hours
  • Platinum Guardian: 70+ confirmed hours

Donor Recognition Tiers

Donors earn 1 point per ₹100 contributed to verified causes. Donations remain transparent while respecting anonymous mode settings.

  • Community Supporter: Up to ₹2,500
  • Cause Patron: ₹2,500 – ₹10,000
  • Impact Champion: ₹10,000 – ₹50,000
  • Ecosystem Guardian: ₹50,000+

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

ImpactHub collects only necessary information to deliver civic volunteering and donation services: name, email address, city, role selection, and optional profile bio. We never store credit card or sensitive banking data; all transactions are handled via compliant payment adapters.

2. Role Isolation & Public Visibility

Leaderboards display only display names and earned points. Email addresses and contact details are never disclosed publicly. Donors may choose to keep their donations entirely anonymous on campaign walls while preserving their private reward progression.

3. Data Export & Account Deletion

Users retain full ownership of their personal data. Through the account settings panel, you can download a complete JSON archive of your activity or permanently delete your account. To maintain legal auditability under Section 80G tax regulations, donation receipt records are anonymized upon account deletion.
